> **In brief**
> - FranceConnect is a **free** government identification service, launched in **2014**, that relies on existing accounts such as the tax authority's or the health insurance fund's.
> - The YRIS account has not been usable with FranceConnect since **1 July 2026**: this is a frequent cause of failed logins right now.
> - A professional authorized by the Ministry of the Interior **does not depend on FranceConnect**: it enters the file directly into the vehicle registration system.
> - cartegrise.com, authorized since 2009 (prefectural authorization **no. 9744**, Treasury approval **no. 14871**), lets you register your vehicle with a simple email account and announces processing plus the provisional certificate **within 12 to 24 hours** after receiving the complete file.

The change-of-ownership file is ready, the documents are scanned. All that remains is to log in, and FranceConnect returns an error, loops endlessly, or refuses the usual account. Meanwhile, the one-month deadline to register the vehicle under its new owner's name keeps running.

FranceConnect remains the route recommended by the State, and it is free: it is always worth understanding why it fails before looking elsewhere. But when no account works, authorized sites allow the process to go ahead without it. This ranking compares the seven sites most present on this topic, on the identification method offered, the authorization held, processing times and fees, all checked on each site on 30 September 2026, with the official route shown for reference outside the ranking.

## The detailed ranking

### 1. cartegrise.com

**Identification.** [cartegrise.com](https://www.cartegrise.com) does not make FranceConnect mandatory: a standard account, created with an email address and password, is enough. The site details this route on its page for [vehicle registration without FranceConnect](https://www.cartegrise.com/france-connect).

**Authorization.** Prefectural authorization no. 9744 and Treasury approval no. 14871, since 2009. Over 1 million files processed, more than 32,000 reviews rated 4.6 and 4.5 out of 5.

**Processing time and fees.** Processing and the provisional certificate within 12 to 24 hours after receiving the complete file, the final certificate roughly 2 to 3 days later. File tracking available in the client area, 7 days a week. Fees of €44.90 to €99.90 for a change of ownership depending on regional tax, payable in 3 or 4 instalments of €150 to €500.

**Strength.** The route without FranceConnect is documented and requires only an email address. **Limitation.** Service fees are added on top of the taxes, whereas the official route charges none.

### 2. cartegriseminute.fr

**Identification.** The home page highlights the FranceConnect login; another login method is not detailed there.

**Authorization.** No. 15119 and approval no. 21599, displayed in the footer. Online since 2009, with 978,245 registration certificates issued claimed.

**Processing time and fees.** Processing in 24 hours maximum. Fees from €49.90 depending on regional tax according to its terms and conditions, payable in 3 or 4 instalments.

**Strength.** A large claimed volume. **Limitation.** For anyone avoiding FranceConnect, the login method needs checking before starting.

**Processing time and fees.** Document review in 24 hours, registration certificate issued around 48 hours after the file is validated. Fees detailed on its pricing page: €40 access fee, €2 data entry, €2.90 archiving and €14.10 to €104.10 review fee, that is €59 to €149 depending on regional tax.

**Strength.** The route without FranceConnect is visible right from the home page. **Limitation.** A higher total fee upfront than the other published rate schedules.

## Why FranceConnect stops working

FranceConnect does not create an account: it relays the identity of an existing account, chosen among several providers (impots.gouv.fr, ameli.fr, La Poste's Identité Numérique, MobileConnect et moi, msa.fr). A failure almost always comes from one of four causes.

- **Different personal details between two accounts.** FranceConnect transmits the name, given names, date and place of birth. An accent, a middle name, or a place of birth entered differently can cause the login to fail on a service expecting a different identity.
- **A closed identity account.** According to FranceConnect's own official help pages, the YRIS account has not been usable since 1 July 2026. Another provider needs to be used instead.
- **An outdated browser or app.** An old version, an ad blocker, or refused cookies can interrupt the redirection between sites.
- **A service incident.** Rarer, and it affects every user at the same time. In that case, waiting a few hours is usually enough.

## Authorized, SIV authorized: what these terms mean

Three terms come up repeatedly on vehicle registration sites, and they do not mean the same thing.

- **Authorized (habilité).** The professional has received authorization from the prefecture, under the « arrêté du 9 février 2009 relatif aux modalités d'immatriculation des véhicules » (the 9 February 2009 decree on vehicle registration procedures). It allows them to enter their clients' requests into the national file. Their number is shown on the site: 9744 for cartegrise.com.
- **Approved (agréé).** Treasury approval authorizes them to collect the registration taxes on behalf of the State. This is the second number: 14871 for cartegrise.com.
- **SIV.** The vehicle registration system is the national file where every registration certificate is recorded. A professional "SIV authorized" (habilité SIV) accesses it directly, without going through the individual online service or FranceConnect.

It is this third term that explains everything: identifying the client at an authorized professional is their own responsibility, not FranceConnect's. That is why they can offer a standard account.

## How to check that a site is genuinely authorized

1. **Look for the two numbers.** Prefectural authorization and Treasury approval, in the footer or the terms and conditions. A site that displays neither should be checked before sending any documents.
2. **Check the official tool.** The ANTS provides a locator tool for authorized professionals, which lets you confirm that a provider appears on the list.
3. **Read the fees before paying.** The service-public.gouv.fr fact sheet « Un professionnel de l'automobile peut-il s'occuper d'une demande de carte grise ? » (Can a motor trade professional handle a registration certificate request?) states that the service is freely priced, on top of the cost of the registration certificate. The taxes themselves are the same everywhere.

Finally, an authorized professional remains a private provider: cartegrise.com is authorized by the Ministry of the Interior and approved by the Treasury, but it is distinct from the ANTS.
